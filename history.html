History of Mathematics

Timeline

  1. c. 20,000 BCE Ishango bone: tally marks in Central Africa.
  2. c. 1800 BCE Babylonians use base 60 (Plimpton 322).
  3. c. 300 BCE Euclid writes the Elements.
  4. 628 CE Brahmagupta sets rules for zero.
  5. c. 820 CE Al-Khwarizmi writes the book of al-jabr.
  6. 1642 Pascal builds a mechanical calculator.
  7. 1687 Newton publishes the Principia.
  8. 1843 Ada Lovelace publishes the first program.

Key figures

Pythagoras (c. 570–495 BCE)

Greek thinker whose school studied number and proof. The theorem a²+b²=c² bears his name, though Babylonians knew it earlier.

Al-Khwarizmi (c. 780–850 CE)

Baghdad scholar whose book on al-jabr gave us the word algebra. His name gave us algorithm.

Isaac Newton (1643–1727)

Developed calculus and the laws of motion and gravity. Leibniz found calculus independently.

Archimedes (c. 287–212 BCE)

Found the volume of a sphere, estimated π with polygons and explained levers and buoyancy.
